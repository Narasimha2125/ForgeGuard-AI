Explainable Scoring Methodology

ForgeGuard AI uses a transparent, bounded deterministic scoring algorithm instead of an opaque black-box score:

1. Severity Weighting Critical = 10 pts, High = 7 pts, Medium = 4 pts, Low = 1 pt. Scaled by detection confidence (0.5 to 1.0).
2. Component Weighting Security exploits represent 45% of total score, third-party dependency vulnerabilities 30%, and code quality anti-patterns 25%.
3. Health Inversion Project Health Score = 100 - Composite Risk. Rated Excellent (90-100), Good (70-89), Needs Improvement (50-69), or Poor (<50).
